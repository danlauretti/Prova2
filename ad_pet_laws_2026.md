# Abu Dhabi Pet Laws & Regulations 2026

## Administrative Decision No. 23 of 2025: Pet-Friendly Tourism Facilities

### Overview
In December 2025, the Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT) approved amendments to tourism regulations allowing licensed hotels and restaurants across Abu Dhabi to welcome companion animals (cats and dogs).

### Key Points
- **Effective Date**: December 2025
- **Issuing Authority**: Department of Municipalities and Transport (DMT)
- **Scope**: Tourism-licensed hotels and restaurants throughout Abu Dhabi Emirate

### What Changed
**Previous Regulations**: Restaurants and most public venues prohibited pets entirely, allowing only certified service animals.

**New Regulations**: Tourism-licensed venues can **voluntarily opt-in** to welcome companion animals, provided they meet operational requirements.

### Requirements for Pet-Friendly Venues

#### Designated Areas
Venues choosing to admit companion animals must allocate specific areas, including:
- **Primary**: Open-air spaces (outdoor seating, patios, balconies, gardens)
- **Secondary**: Suitable indoor or enclosed zones (at venue's discretion)

#### Companion Animal Definition
"Companion animals" refers to pets that typically accompany their owners outside the home, specifically:
- Dogs
- Cats

### Purpose & Goals
The amendments aim to:
- Promote inclusivity for pet owners
- Enhance Abu Dhabi's standing as a world-leading destination
- Reinforce exceptional hospitality standards
- Support the growing pet-owning community

### Participation
- **Voluntary**: Individual hotels and restaurants decide whether to implement pet-friendly policies
- **No Mandate**: Businesses are not required to accept pets
- **Recommended**: Contact venues in advance to confirm pet policies and any size restrictions

## Mandatory TAMM Pet Registration (2026)

### Registration Timeline & Deadlines

#### Individual Pet Owners
- **Start Date**: February 3, 2025
- **Grace Period Ends**: February 3, 2026
- **Penalty Phase Begins**: January 2026 (fines to be enforced for non-compliance)

#### Pet-Owning Establishments
- **Deadline**: 6 months from February 3, 2025 (approximately August 2025)
- **Scope**: Businesses, kennels, shelters, and other organizations owning cats or dogs

### Registration Requirements

All pet owners must register **microchipped cats and dogs** through the Animal Ownership System.

#### What You Need
1. **Identity Documents**
   - Emirates ID
   - Access to UAE Pass (National Digital Identity)
   - Access to TAMM (Abu Dhabi Government Services platform)

2. **Pet Documentation**
   - Proof of ownership (e.g., Pet Passport, adoption papers)
   - Proof of residence in Abu Dhabi
   - Current health check-up records
   - Vaccination records (up to date)
   - Microchip identification (required before registration)

### Registration Process

1. **Microchip Your Pet** (if not already done)
   - Required for identification purposes
   - Available at licensed veterinary clinics

2. **Book Veterinary Appointment**
   - Visit nearest licensed veterinary clinic
   - Clinic staff will assist with the registration process
   - Bring all required documentation

3. **Complete Online Registration**
   - Access via TAMM platform: [tamm.abudhabi](https://www.tamm.abudhabi/en/life-events/individual/CultureTourism/pet-care/RequesttoRegisteranAnimalProfile)
   - Use UAE Pass for authentication
   - Submit required documents digitally

4. **Verification**
   - DMT will verify the information
   - Registration confirmation will be issued

### Fees
- **Current Fee**: No registration fee (as of January 2026)
- **Future Changes**: Monitor TAMM for potential fee updates

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- **Enforcement**: Starting January 2026
- **Fines**: Specific amounts to be announced by DMT
- **Consequences**: Potential fines for unregistered pets

### Purpose of Registration
The mandatory pet registration system aims to:
- **Control stray animal population**
- **Reduce risk of disease transmission**
- **Ensure animal welfare standards**
- **Enable reunification of lost pets with owners**
- **Track pet ownership for public health and safety**

## Best Practices for Pet Owners in Abu Dhabi

### When Visiting Pet-Friendly Venues
1. **Call Ahead**: Always confirm pet policy, especially regarding size restrictions
2. **Leash Required**: Keep pets leashed in public areas
3. **Clean Up**: Always clean up after your pet
4. **Vaccinations**: Ensure pets are up-to-date on vaccinations
5. **Behavior**: Ensure your pet is well-behaved and socialized

### Registration Compliance
1. **Register Early**: Don't wait until the February 2026 deadline
2. **Keep Records Updated**: Update TAMM if you move or change contact details
3. **Microchip First**: Ensure microchipping before attempting registration
4. **Retain Documentation**: Keep copies of registration confirmation

### Health & Safety
1. **Regular Vet Visits**: Maintain health check-ups and vaccinations
2. **Hydration**: Carry water for your pet, especially in warm weather
3. **Heat Awareness**: Avoid outdoor activities during peak heat hours
4. **Emergency Contacts**: Know location of 24/7 emergency veterinary clinics
